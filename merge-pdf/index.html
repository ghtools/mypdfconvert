Frequently Asked Questions

How can I merge PDF files online for free?

Choose two or more PDF files above, reorder them if needed, and tap Merge PDFs. The combined file downloads directly to your device.

Is this PDF merger really private?

Yes. Merging happens entirely inside your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server.

Can I merge PDFs without uploading them?

Yes. Your files stay on your device the entire time — nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I merge more than two PDFs at once?

Yes. You can select as many PDF files as you like — add them one at a time or all together, then reorder them before merging.

Can I change the order of the pages before merging?

Yes. After choosing your files, drag and drop them in the list to set the order they'll appear in the final PDF.

Will merging reduce the quality of my PDFs?

No. Merging simply combines the original pages as-is, with no re-compression or quality loss.

Can I merge PDFs on Android?

Yes. This tool works in any modern mobile browser on Android, including Chrome and Firefox.

Can I merge PDFs on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad without installing an app.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on both desktop and mobile.

Does this work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Since it runs in your browser, it works the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

Is there a limit to how many files I can merge?

There's no fixed limit — the only constraint is your device's available memory, since everything runs in your browser.

Is this the best free way to merge PDFs fast?

It's a strong private option — most mergers upload your files to a server, while this one processes them entirely on your device.
