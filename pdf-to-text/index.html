About This Tool

Extract text from a PDF online, completely free, without uploading your file anywhere. This browser-based tool pulls the readable text out of your document into a plain .txt file, ready to copy, edit, or search. Because everything runs locally in your browser, your file stays private and secure the entire time — nothing is sent to a server. It works smoothly on mobile and desktop, including Android, iPhone, Windows, and Mac, in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari. It works best on text-based PDFs; scanned or image-based PDFs don't contain extractable text. No signup, no watermark, no limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I extract text from a PDF online for free?

Choose your PDF above and tap Extract Text. A plain .txt file downloads directly to your device.

Is this PDF text extractor really private?

Yes. Extraction happens entirely inside your browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server.

Can I extract text without uploading my PDF?

Yes. Your PDF stays on your device the entire time — nothing is sent anywhere.

Why is my extracted text file empty?

This happens when the PDF is scanned or made up of images rather than real text. Only text actually stored as text in the file can be extracted, not text inside an image.

Does this preserve formatting like bold or tables?

No. The output is plain text only — formatting, fonts, and layout are not preserved, only the words themselves.

Can I extract text from scanned documents?

Not with this tool. Scanned PDFs need OCR to detect text within images, which this tool does not currently perform.

Can I extract PDF text on Android?

Yes. This tool works in any modern mobile browser on Android, including Chrome and Firefox.

Can I extract PDF text on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad without installing an app.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on both desktop and mobile.

Does this work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Since it runs in your browser, it works the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

Is this the best free way to extract PDF text fast?

It's a strong private option — most extractors upload your file to a server, while this one processes it entirely on your device.

Do I need to sign up to extract PDF text?

No signup, no account, and no email address required.
