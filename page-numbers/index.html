Frequently Asked Questions

How can I add page numbers to a PDF online for free?

Choose your PDF above, pick a position, format, and starting number, then tap Add Page Numbers. The result downloads directly to your device.

Is this page numbering tool really private?

Yes. Page numbers are added entirely inside your browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server.

Can I choose where the page numbers appear?

Yes. Choose bottom center, bottom left, bottom right, top center, top left, or top right.

Can I use "Page X of Y" format?

Yes. Choose the "Page X of Y" format to show both the current page and the total page count.

Can I start numbering from something other than 1?

Yes. Enter any starting number, useful when this document continues from another one.

Does numbering apply to every page?

Yes, sequential numbers are added to every page of the document.

Can I add page numbers on Android?

Yes. This tool works in any modern mobile browser on Android, including Chrome and Firefox.

Can I add page numbers on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad without installing an app.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on both desktop and mobile.

Does this work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Since it runs in your browser, it works the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

Is this the best free way to number PDF pages fast?

It's a strong private option — most tools upload your file to a server, while this one processes it entirely on your device.

Do I need to sign up to add page numbers?
