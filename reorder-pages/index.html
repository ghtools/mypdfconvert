Frequently Asked Questions

How can I reorder PDF pages online for free?

Choose your PDF above, type the new page order separated by commas, and tap Reorder Pages. The result downloads directly to your device.

Is this page reorder tool really private?

Yes. Reordering happens entirely inside your browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server.

Do I need to include every page in the new order?

Yes. Every page number from 1 to the total page count must appear exactly once in your new order.

What if I only want to move one page?

List all pages in the order you want, moving just the one page. For example, to move page 5 to the front of a 6-page document, enter "5,1,2,3,4,6".

Can I reorder pages on Android?

Yes. This tool works in any modern mobile browser on Android, including Chrome and Firefox.

Can I reorder pages on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad without installing an app.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on both desktop and mobile.

Does this work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Since it runs in your browser, it works the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

Will reordering affect page quality?

No. Pages are simply rearranged as-is, with no re-compression or quality loss.

What if I want to remove a page instead of reordering?

Use the Remove Pages tool instead, which lets you delete specific pages.

Is this the best free way to reorder PDF pages fast?

It's a strong private option — most tools upload your file to a server, while this one processes it entirely on your device.

Do I need to sign up to reorder pages?

No signup, no account, and no email address required.
