Frequently Asked Questions

How can I rotate a PDF online for free?

Choose your PDF above, pick a rotation angle, and tap Rotate PDF. The corrected file downloads directly to your device.

Is this PDF rotator really private?

Yes. Rotation happens entirely inside your browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server.

Can I rotate a PDF without uploading it?

Yes. Your PDF stays on your device the entire time — nothing is sent anywhere.

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. Rotation only changes the page orientation metadata — the underlying content is untouched.

Can I rotate a PDF on Android?

Yes. This tool works in any modern mobile browser on Android, including Chrome and Firefox.

Can I rotate a PDF on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad without installing an app.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on both desktop and mobile.

Does this work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Since it runs in your browser, it works the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

How do I know which direction to rotate?

If text reads bottom-to-top, use 90° clockwise. If it reads top-to-bottom, use 90° counter-clockwise. If it's fully upside down, use 180°.

Does rotation apply to every page?

Yes, the chosen rotation is applied to every page in the file.

Is this the best free way to rotate a PDF fast?

It's a strong private option — most rotators upload your file to a server, while this one processes it entirely on your device.

Do I need to sign up to rotate a PDF?

No signup, no account, and no email address required.
