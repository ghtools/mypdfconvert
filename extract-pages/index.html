Frequently Asked Questions

How can I extract pages from a PDF online for free?

Choose your PDF above, type the page numbers to keep, and tap Extract Pages. The result downloads directly to your device.

Is this PDF page extractor really private?

Yes. Pages are extracted entirely inside your browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server.

Can I extract pages without uploading my PDF?

Yes. Your PDF stays on your device the entire time — nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I enter which pages to extract?

Type page numbers separated by commas, and use a hyphen for a range. For example, "1, 3, 5-7" extracts page 1, page 3, and pages 5 through 7.

What order will the extracted pages be in?

They'll appear in the order you type them, so you can also use this tool to reorder select pages.

Can I extract pages on Android?

Yes. This tool works in any modern mobile browser on Android, including Chrome and Firefox.

Can I extract pages on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad without installing an app.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on both desktop and mobile.

Does this work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Since it runs in your browser, it works the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

What if I want to delete pages instead of keeping them?

Use the Remove Pages tool instead — it lets you specify which pages to delete rather than which to keep.

Is this the best free way to extract PDF pages fast?

It's a strong private option — most tools upload your file to a server, while this one processes it entirely on your device.

Do I need to sign up to extract pages?

No signup, no account, and no email address required.
