About This Tool

Convert PDF to Word online, completely free, without uploading your file anywhere. Frequently Asked Questions

How can I convert PDF to Word online for free?

Choose your PDF above and tap Convert to Word. An editable .docx file downloads directly to your device.

Is this PDF to Word converter really private?

Yes. Conversion happens entirely inside your browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server.

Will my formatting, images, and tables be preserved?

No. This tool extracts the readable text from your PDF and places it into a real, editable .docx file. Layout, fonts, images, and tables are not preserved — only the text content.

Will the file open in Microsoft Word?

Yes. The output is a standard .docx file that opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice, and most other word processors, and the text is fully editable.

Does this work on scanned PDFs?

No. Scanned or image-based PDFs don't contain real text to extract. This tool works best on PDFs created from Word, Google Docs, or similar text-based sources.

Can I convert PDF to Word on Android?

Yes. This tool works in any modern mobile browser on Android, including Chrome and Firefox.

Can I convert PDF to Word on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad without installing an app.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on both desktop and mobile.

Does this work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Since it runs in your browser, it works the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

Why isn't full formatting supported?

Accurately recreating a PDF's exact layout in Word requires heavy server-side processing. To keep this tool free, private, and running entirely in your browser, we focus on reliably extracting the text instead.

Is this the best free way to convert PDF to Word fast?

It's a strong private option — most converters upload your file to a server, while this one processes it entirely on your device.

Do I need to sign up to convert PDF to Word?

No signup, no account, and no email address required.
