About This Tool

Convert Word to PDF online, completely free, without uploading your document anywhere. This browser-based tool reads the text of your .docx file and lays it out as plain paragraphs in a new, standard PDF. Because everything runs locally in your browser, your document stays private and secure the entire time — nothing is sent to a server. It works smoothly on mobile and desktop, including Android, iPhone, Windows, and Mac, in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari. It works best on text-based documents; layout, fonts, and images are not preserved. No signup, no watermark, no limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I convert Word to PDF online for free?

Choose your .docx file above and tap Convert to PDF. The PDF downloads directly to your device.

Is this Word to PDF converter really private?

Yes. Conversion happens entirely inside your browser. Your document is never uploaded to a server.

Will my formatting, images, and tables be preserved?

No. This tool extracts the text from your Word document and lays it out as plain paragraphs in a new PDF. Fonts, images, tables, and complex layout are not preserved.

What file types are supported?

Only .docx files (the modern Word format) are supported. Older .doc files are not supported.

Can I convert Word to PDF on Android?

Yes. This tool works in any modern mobile browser on Android, including Chrome and Firefox.

Can I convert Word to PDF on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad without installing an app.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on both desktop and mobile.

Does this work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Since it runs in your browser, it works the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

Why isn't full formatting supported?

Accurately recreating a Word document's exact layout requires heavy server-side processing. To keep this tool free, private, and running entirely in your browser, we focus on reliably converting the text instead.

Is this the best free way to convert Word to PDF fast?

It's a strong private option — most converters upload your document to a server, while this one processes it entirely on your device.

Do I need to sign up to convert Word to PDF?

No signup, no account, and no email address required.

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion happens entirely in your browser — your file is never uploaded to a server.
