Frequently Asked Questions

How can I add a watermark to a PDF online for free?

Choose your PDF above, type your watermark text, pick a position and color, and tap Add Watermark. The result downloads directly to your device.

Is this PDF watermark tool really private?

Yes. The watermark is added entirely inside your browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server.

Can I choose where the watermark appears?

Yes. Choose from center (diagonal or horizontal), or any of the four corners.

Can I change the color?

Yes. Choose gray, red, blue, or black, and adjust the opacity to make it subtle or bold.

Does the watermark appear on every page?

Yes, the same watermark is applied to every page of the document.

Can I add a watermark on Android?

Yes. This tool works in any modern mobile browser on Android, including Chrome and Firefox.

Can I add a watermark on iPhone?

Yes. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad without installing an app.

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on both desktop and mobile.

Does this work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Since it runs in your browser, it works the same on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

Can I remove a watermark I've already added?

Not with this tool — once a watermark is stamped onto the page content, it becomes part of the page itself.

Is this the best free way to add a PDF watermark fast?
