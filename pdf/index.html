Why Crop a PDF

  • Remove excess white space from scanned pages
  • Focus the page on the content that matters
  • Prepare a document for a specific print size
  • Trim unwanted headers or footers
  • Make slides or images fill the page better

How to Crop PDF Online

  2. Set the percentage to trim from each side
  3. Tap "Crop PDF"
  4. Download your cropped PDF

Yes. Your PDF stays on your device the entire time — nothing is sent anywhere.

Cropping adjusts the visible page area. Most PDF viewers and printers will only show the cropped region, giving the effect of trimmed margins.
